# Brag Plan: Studium

## What is this app?
Studium is a lightweight Electron desktop browser built for PDF-heavy academic study sessions — it uses ~2× less RAM than Edge on the same PDF, enforces a hard 5-tab limit, and strips everything non-essential with Study Mode and Reader Mode.

## The angle
This isn't a joke product — it's an earnest engineering project that solves a real problem every student has: your browser eating all your RAM while you're trying to read a PDF. The confidence comes from the benchmarks. The hook is the absurdity of needing a *whole new browser* just to read a PDF without your laptop catching fire. The video should feel like a quiet, assured product reveal — the numbers speak for themselves.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant text: "Your browser uses 900 MB to read a PDF." — a true, infuriating fact that every student recognizes. Teal accent glow fades in behind the text. This earns the next 17 seconds by making the viewer nod.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Studium product frame (real screenshot from the project) slides in with the three traffic-light dots — clean, minimal browser chrome showing a PDF open in study mode.
- The benchmark bar chart: Studium 220 MB vs Edge 390 MB vs Chrome 475 MB — the bars animate up, Studium's teal bar stays tiny while the others tower. The numbers lock in.
- "5-tab hard limit" — the constraint stated as a feature, with the three trust badges (Open Source · MIT Licensed · No Telemetry) appearing beneath.

## Outro / punchline
"Studium" logo + tagline: "A browser for focus, not clutter." — the hero headline from the site, verbatim. Clean hold on the dark background. No CTA. Confidence.

## User flow worth showing
None — landing-page only. Rely on the benchmark visual and product frame screenshot instead.
